# Overview
The practice of Dr. Jamila Brown is defined by "Warm Minimalism". The design philosophy is centered around the "Affirming Guide" — providing a safe, trust-inducing, and professional space for clinical care. The aesthetic is "Organic & Calm".

# Visuals & Specs
- **Mood:** Affirming, Warm, Professional, Safe, Organic.
- **Imagery:** Soft-focus natural elements, warm interiors, professional yet approachable portraits.

# Components & Elements
- **Safe Containers:** Soft-edged, sand-colored containers that "hold" the content.
- **Affirming Buttons:** Sage green buttons with generous padding and soft (8px) radius.
- **Organic Accents:** Subtle use of botanical or natural line art to reinforce the "organic" feel.
- **Clear Forms:** Simple, high-contrast consultation forms that minimize friction and anxiety.

# State & Logic
- **Interaction Feedback:** Buttons shift to a slightly deeper sage on hover to provide gentle confirmation.
- **Content Unfolding:** Information is presented in logical, digestible chunks to avoid overwhelming the visitor.
- **Warm Light:** The aesthetic is exclusively light-mode, focusing on transparency and openness.
