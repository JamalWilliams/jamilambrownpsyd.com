# Expat Uncertainty

**Moving away from home is a big decision.**

Moving overseas? Living abroad? Difficulty adjusting? Missing family and friends? Questioning if you made the right decision?

If you are a digital nomad, professional traveler, or someone who has relocated, having a space to speak to a professional about your experience in English can provide significant relief.
