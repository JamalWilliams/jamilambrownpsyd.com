# Individual Therapy

**Feeling sad, anxious or just unsure about your next step?**

Individual therapy can help you take control of your situation and move forward. Whether you are dealing with depression, anxiety, or general life transitions, our work together focuses on:

- Gaining greater understanding and compassion towards yourself
- Improving self-esteem and self-confidence
- Reducing stress, worry, and anger
- Enhancing communication skills
- Improving problem-solving abilities
- Reaching your personal goals
- Strengthening your relationships
