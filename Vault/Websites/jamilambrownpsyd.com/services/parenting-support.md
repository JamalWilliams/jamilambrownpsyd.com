# Parenting Support

**Every child is different.**

Questions about discipline, behavior problems or sleep disturbances? Learn to be your child's advocate in the school system. Blending families or co-parenting a step child can be difficult at times; therapy can help you move through these transitions.

As a trained Parent Child Interaction Therapy (PCIT) coach, I provide evidence-based strategies to help you strengthen your relationship with your child while effectively managing challenging behaviors.
