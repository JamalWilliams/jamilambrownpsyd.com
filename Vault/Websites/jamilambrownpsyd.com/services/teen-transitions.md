# Teen Transitions

**Growing up & letting go.**

The teen years can be a difficult time for everyone in the family. Having someone to talk to can help. Many teens find online therapy more comfortable and accessible than a traditional setting. 

Family sessions are also available to support improved communication and understanding within the household.
