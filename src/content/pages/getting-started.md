# The First Step: Getting Started

Experience professional psychological support from the comfort of your own space.

## Consultation
Schedule a free 15-minute consultation to discuss your needs and see if we are a good fit.

## Convenience & Comfort
- **Work around your schedule**: From your home or office. Online therapy takes less time away from your workday. No need to worry about traffic or parking.
- **Safe Space**: Online therapy is a supportive option for those in complex situations, teens, or individuals with chronic illnesses or disabilities.
- **Location Independent**: Access therapy from anywhere in the world. Perfect for digital nomads, professional travelers, or those living abroad wishing to have therapy in their native language.
